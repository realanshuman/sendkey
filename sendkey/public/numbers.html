What we cannot count

Who sent anything, who opened it, what any of it said, or which secret was a file rather than text. The server stores ciphertext it cannot read and keeps no per-secret records beyond the burn receipt each sender can already see. These totals are the whole dataset: there is no second, more detailed one.
