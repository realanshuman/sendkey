Ask for a secret

Flip the direction. You request, they answer.

Your browser will generate a keypair. The public half rides the link you share; the private half never leaves this browser. Whatever gets sent back can only be opened here.

  • Send the link to whoever holds the secret
  • They answer straight into an encrypted mailbox
  • You open it here. It burns on reading, as always.
