Sonara · clinical dashboard

An instrument panel, not an app.

This system turns the Sonara brand kit into working parts for the product a doctor uses forty-five times a day. The bar is legibility across a desk on a three-year-old Windows monitor: paper-calm surfaces, one saturated red that only ever means "the mic is live", and type a 41-year-old can read at arm's length. Tokens are mirrored in tokens.css.

Principles

Five rules that outrank every component on this page.

01

The doctor signs

Nothing clinical is ever presented as decided. Drafts look like drafts, suggestions carry their source, and the only primary-colored action on a finished note is the one that makes the doctor read it.

02

Signal means live

One saturated red exists in the interface and it means a microphone is capturing this room. It pulses; nothing else ever pulses red. Errors and hard stops use Brick, which is darker and never animates.

03

Say the thing

Every state is written in plain words: "Saving locally — nothing is lost." A spinner without a sentence is a bug. Sentence case everywhere; Title Case reads as marketing.

04

Paper calm

The room is stressful; the screen is not allowed to be. Chalk cards on paper, one accent, no gradients, shadows only where something floats. Density comes from type discipline, not from shrinking text.

05

Keyboard first

Hands are on a keyboard between patients. Everything reachable by Tab, visible focus on everything, and the common actions carry shortcuts. Hit targets never shrink below 40px.

The app shell

Four columns, one job each. The rail navigates, the queue is the clinic day, the canvas is the current visit, the aside is the patient's memory. On smaller screens the aside folds first, then the queue becomes a drawer.

rail 64
queue 272
canvas · flex
aside 320
The rail is the only Ink surface in the working screen — a fixed dark edge that anchors the layout without darkening the room. The canvas alone scrolls; the queue and aside scroll their own lists. The patient header (name, age, flags) is sticky over the canvas and can never be collapsed while a visit is open.

Motion

Motion states facts; it never decorates. Three durations, and a total ban on movement the doctor didn't cause — with two named exceptions.

Status & flags

Two different vocabularies. Status chips track a visit through the day. Flag chips are clinical facts on the patient header — they can never be collapsed, truncated or hidden while a visit is open.

don'tLet flags scroll away. The header they live on is sticky for exactly this reason.

Consent module

Legally load-bearing. The checkbox arms the record button; nothing else does. Consent is per visit — it always loads unticked — and the script the doctor reads sits in the open, in the patient's language.

"I'd like to record our conversation so your notes are written properly. The recording stays private and is deleted on a schedule. Is that okay?"

The record button stays off until consent is ticked.
doOffer the manual path at equal rank when consent is refused — "Write the note without recording" is a first-class button, not a fallback link.
doStore language, method and timestamp with the consent record; show "asked in Marathi at 11:21" on the visit afterwards.
don'tPre-tick, remember across visits, or hide the script behind a tooltip. Ever.

Recording

The waveform is an instrument reading, not decoration — it must reflect the real input level, because a flat line is how the doctor discovers a dead mic before losing a consult to it.

● Recording · 04:12Mic: default — Realtek
Idle · armed
Flat — check the mic
No sound is reaching Sonara. Check the Windows sound settings, then reload. The visit can continue — write the note manually if needed.
doWarn at 20 minutes, auto-stop at 45 — with a banner, never silently. Audio is always kept.
doKeep recording through network loss: buffer locally and say so in words ("Saving locally — will upload when the connection returns").

Transcript

Speaker-tagged, timestamped, code-mix left exactly as spoken. Low-confidence words get a dotted amber underline instead of silent smoothing. Tap a line to correct it — corrections feed the clinic's own vocabulary, nobody else's.

DR00:04Haan Priya, batao — kya taqleef ho rahi hai?
PT00:07Do din se bukhar hai, and throat is paining a lot.
DR00:26Throat dekhte hain… erythema present, tonsils mildly enlarged. Temp 100.8.
— paused 00:41–02:10 · phone call —
ATT02:12Doctor, raat ko saans lene mein bhi taqleef thi.
The highlighted line shows provenance: hovering a sentence in the drafted note lights up the transcript lines it came from. The green wash is the same one used for selection everywhere.
doOffer "Swap DR ↔ PT" as a single action when the heuristic guesses wrong. One tap, whole transcript.
don'tTranslate or clean the transcript. It is evidence; the note is the interpretation.

Prescription composer

In V1 the doctor composes everything — Sonara autocompletes from a licensed drug database, checks allergies, formats, and delivers. Free text is allowed with an amber warning; an allergy match is a Brick hard stop.

doResolve every entry to salt + strength + form from the licensed database. The database is licensed data, never generated.
doKeep the sign action showing the full final content — the confirmation sheet renders the prescription exactly as the patient receives it.
don'tShip any path where an unsigned prescription reaches print, WhatsApp or email. This is a database invariant, not a UI convention.

Banners & toasts

Every banner is a full sentence with the consequence included. The offline banner is Ink — calm, factual, impossible to miss — because losing a consult is the one unforgivable failure.

Priya's record moved. This visit is now filed under Priya Nair · PT-40219.
Recording has been running 20 minutes. It will stop by itself at 45 — nothing is discarded.

Modals & sheets

Two special forms matter more than the generic dialog: the allergy hard stop, and the signing sheet. Try them live.

doMake the override cost deliberate effort: a typed reason, minimum 10 characters, logged with the doctor's name in the audit trail.
doRender the signing sheet as the actual artifact — the doctor sees the identical content the patient will hold, plus their own signature block.
don'tSummarize at signing time ("3 medicines, 1 note"). Counts hide mistakes; content exposes them.

Safety patterns

The §10 invariants, as interface law. These are the rules a redesign is not allowed to touch.

InvariantWhat the interface does
Consent gates the micRecord is disabled until the per-visit checkbox; refusing consent routes to an equal-rank manual note. Withdrawal stops recording and schedules deletion, stated on screen.
Only the doctor prescribesRx state is visibly unsigned until the signing sheet; sign is blocked for unverified registrations with the reason shown; the sheet renders full content, never a summary.
Signed means immutableA signed note locks — fields lose their edit affordance entirely. Corrections happen as addenda with author, time and reason; a corrected Rx is a new Rx referencing the original.
Roles are wallsFront desk sees the queue and demographics; clinical panels don't render at all for them — no disabled previews, no teasers. Blocked reads still get audited.
Honest uncertaintyLow-confidence sections carry the amber banner; unclear words are dotted, never smoothed; a thin section stays thin.
Connection honestyOffline is a named state ("Saving locally"), not a spinner. The doctor's next action is never blocked on a round trip.

Accessibility

The primary user is over 40, reading across a desk, on Chrome or Edge on Windows. These are requirements, not aspirations.

rule16px floor. Body text, inputs and note fields never render below 16px. 14px exists only for metadata; 11–12px only for mono labels.
ruleFocus is visible everywhere: 2.5px Sea outline, 2px offset, on every interactive element including contenteditable fields and table rows.
ruleKeyboard-complete: the core loop — pick patient, consent, record, end, edit, sign — works without a pointer. / focuses search, Esc closes sheets, arrows walk the queue.
ruleState never rides on color alone: every status chip pairs its color with a word; the live states also pulse; the flat-mic state adds a banner.
ruleHit targets ≥40px on anything tapped during a consult — the doctor is not aiming carefully while a patient talks.
ruleReduced motion honored globally; the recording state remains distinguishable as a solid Signal dot plus the word.

Reserved for V1.5

Drug suggestions ship only after real notes exist to ground them. The component is specified now so V1.5 doesn't invent a new visual language: a suggestion is a proposal with a visible source, never an auto-added line.

Hold the antibiotic

Presentation reads viral: gradual onset, no exudate noted, dry cough. Nothing added to the draft.

Why → examination line · "dry cough, raat ko zyada" at 00:17
Every suggestion selects from the licensed database, carries a "Why →" trace to a transcript line or record entry, and offers Add / Dismiss. Dismissals are logged; a suggestion type dismissed >70% of the time is removed, not tuned. Withholding advice ("antibiotic not added") is a first-class suggestion.
