Release gates, not marketing claims

Numbers we hold every release to.

Every build runs against a held-out set of real consultations with hand-written gold notes. Below any of these, it doesn't ship — the eval harness was built before the product was.

≤15s
End visit → draft on screen, p95, for a ten-minute consult. Slower than this and the next patient is already talking.
≥95%
Speaker attribution. History belongs to the patient, findings to the doctor. Mixing them corrupts the note.
≤1%
Unsupported clinical assertions. If it wasn't said in the room, it isn't in the note. This gate blocks release.
0
Unsigned prescriptions, ever. Not a policy — a database invariant. No code path produces one.

When audio is unclear, the note says so instead of guessing — a confident wrong note is more dangerous than an honest thin one.

Questions doctors ask first

Asked in every demo. Answered here.

What do I tell the patient?+

One line, on screen, in English, Hindi or Marathi: "I'd like to record our conversation so your notes are written properly. The recording stays private and is deleted on a schedule. Is that okay?" If they decline, the record button stays off and you write the note as you do today — that path is first-class, not a punishment.

What happens when the clinic internet drops?+

Recording never depends on the connection. Audio buffers on your computer and uploads when the line returns; the screen says "Saving locally" so you're never guessing. A backend outage delays your note — it cannot lose your consult.

Does it prescribe?+

No. You compose every prescription. Sonara autocompletes from a licensed drug database, blocks drugs that match a recorded allergy (you can override, with a typed reason that's logged), formats the prescription, and delivers it after you sign. Nothing reaches a patient unsigned — there is no code path for it.

Which languages does it handle?+

Hindi–English and Marathi–English code-mixing at launch, including the mid-sentence switches your patients actually make. The written note is always clinical English. More language pairs follow the same bar: real-consultation evals, not vendor benchmarks.

Where does the audio live, and for how long?+

Encrypted in object storage, accessible only through short-lived signed links, retained for a window you choose (90 days by default) and then purged automatically — each purge writes an audit entry. The signed note is the medical record and is kept per medical-records law; the audio is not the record. No provider in the chain trains on your patients' data — that's contractual.

What does the pilot cost?+

The pilot day costs nothing but the afternoon it takes to set up. Bring your next twenty patients; if the evening write-up hour doesn't disappear, you've lost an afternoon and we've learned something. Pricing after that is per doctor per month — no per-note meter punishing your busiest days.
